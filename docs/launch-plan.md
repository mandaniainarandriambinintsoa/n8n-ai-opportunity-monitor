## 30-day measurement

Review each week:

- referring domains and links in Google Search Console;
- referral sessions by UTM source;
- template page views and copies;
- GitHub views, clones and stars;
- engaged sessions and 75% scroll events in PostHog;
- contact CTA clicks and successful form submissions;
- qualified conversations attributed to the campaign.

Do not evaluate the campaign only by backlink count. A relevant template copy, a qualified conversation or a branded search can be more valuable than several low-quality directory links.
